## Expected Behavior

The README-started local application must support all in-scope demonstration
workflows, including immutable attributed history of lifecycle changes.
The history of a submitted Draft must show its Draft-to-Proposed change,
who submitted it, and when. Deployment and HTTPS are not prerequisites.

## Actual Behavior

The application starts locally and the UI workflows operate, but the rendered
Change history omits every Draft-to-Proposed submission. The UI record was
submitted, edited back to Draft, resubmitted, and accepted; history shows
content edits, Proposed-to-Draft restart, acceptance, version changes,
supersession, and comment deletion, but not submission.
