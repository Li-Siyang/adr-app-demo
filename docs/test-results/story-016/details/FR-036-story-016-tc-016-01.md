## Expected Behavior

With 25 preconfigured Mock identities and 1,000 representative decision
records, every lifecycle transition must create retained audit evidence
identifying what changed, the acting Mock identity, and when. This includes
Draft to Proposed, not merely acceptance, rejection, or review restart.

## Actual Behavior

Submission succeeds and the record becomes Proposed, but no audit entry is
created for Draft to Proposed. A newly created ordinary record's audit
collection remains empty after submission. Other transition categories have
audit entries, so checking only the existence of `lifecycle_transitioned`
would incorrectly conceal the defect.
